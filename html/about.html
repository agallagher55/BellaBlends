Our Story

Bella Blends started in a home kitchen with one goal: make treats we'd feel good giving our own dog. Every batch is made by hand in small quantities, using simple, real ingredients you can actually pronounce - no fillers, no artificial preservatives, no mystery meat.

We bake, dehydrate, and taste-test everything ourselves (well, our resident quality control expert handles the tasting) before it ever makes it into a bag. Today you'll find us at local markets around town and shipping treats straight to porches everywhere.

What We Believe

Real Ingredients

If we wouldn't recognize it on an ingredient label, it doesn't go in the mixing bowl.

Small Batches

We make treats in small runs so everything is fresh - never sitting in a warehouse for months.

Made Locally

Every treat is baked or dehydrated right here, by us, not outsourced to a big-batch factory.
